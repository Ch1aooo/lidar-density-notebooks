Definitions and limitations

All local peaks are retained in the hierarchy. Equal-valued cells activate together under 8-neighbour connectivity. A dying branch uses its connected region strictly above its merge saddle. The essential root has no finite death or excess-mass metric. Root retention is an explicit convention.

Area is grid quadrature area (m²). Integrated density and excess mass have score·m² units (score²·m² for pseudo-energy); neither is physical human volume. Raw support counts original points mapped to the nearest in-domain grid node. Descendant regions may be nested and must not be summed as disjoint measurements.

Single-target scenes are synthetic Gaussian anatomical contributions, not ray-traced LiDAR. The same exploratory thresholds apply to every scene. A false extra mode is a useful negative-control failure. Changing σ recomputes the landscape in Julia; displayed peak IDs are local to each σ, not temporal identities.
