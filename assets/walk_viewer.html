Centroid definitions

Head: mean of returns in the inclusive Z = 1.4–1.6 m band.

Whole body: mean of all saved foreground returns; background residue may remain.

MSP: a Gaussian-density mode estimated from all returns in one frame. XY accumulates height and places the result at Z = 0; XYZ searches full space. It is a candidate observed-density peak, not an anatomical centre, person count or identity.
